LittleLetter Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

LittleLetter (“the App”) is an offline kindergarten English spelling game for iOS, published by Joe.C.

Data we collect

The App does not collect personal data. It does not require an account, does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs, and does not transmit gameplay or device identifiers to our servers.

How the App works

Spelling practice runs on your device. Progress and settings stay on the device. The App is designed to work without a network connection.

Children

The App is intended for young children with a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
